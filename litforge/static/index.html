How to read this experiment

Bits per byte measures how much probability the model gives to the text that actually occurred. It is not an accuracy percentage or a writing-quality score. Parameters and training bytes are matched approximately and exactly, respectively; FLOPs and wall time are not matched. One author, one final test book, and three seeds limit the conclusions. A lower baseline score is a useful result, not something to hide.
